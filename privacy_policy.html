Sweet Fern Cafe Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Sweet Fern Cafe respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and share personal information when you visit our cafe, contact us, place an order, or interact with us online.

  1. Who is responsible for your information? Sweet Fern Cafe is responsible for handling your personal information as described in this policy. If you have questions or would like to exercise your privacy rights, contact us using the contact details shown on our website, booking or order confirmation, or receipt.

  2. Information we may collect Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect: • Contact details, such as your name, email address and telephone number, when you contact us, make a booking or place an order. • Order and transaction details, including items purchased, payment status and relevant booking or delivery information. • Information you choose to provide in messages, feedback, reviews or requests, including information about dietary requirements where you choose to share it. • Technical information, such as your IP address, device and browser details, and information about how you use our website. This may be collected through cookies or similar technologies where applicable.

Please do not provide sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your request.

  1. How we use your information We may use personal information to: • Take and fulfil orders, bookings and payments. • Respond to questions, feedback and requests. • Provide customer service and manage our relationship with you. • Operate, protect and improve our services and website. • Meet legal, accounting and food-safety obligations, and establish or defend legal claims. • Send marketing messages where permitted by law. You can opt out at any time using the unsubscribe option or by contacting us.

  2. Our lawful bases Under UK data protection law, we rely on one or more of the following lawful bases, as applicable: performing a contract with you; complying with a legal obligation; pursuing our legitimate interests in running and improving the cafe, provided those interests do not override your rights; and your consent, where consent is required, including for certain marketing or optional cookies.

  3. Sharing your information We may share information with service providers that help us run the cafe, such as payment processors, booking or ordering platforms, delivery services, website and IT providers, and professional advisers. They may use information only as needed to provide their services and must protect it appropriately. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our rights, customers or business.

  4. International transfers Some service providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where personal information is transferred internationally, we will take steps required by UK data protection law to protect it.

  5. How long we keep information We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including to provide services, maintain appropriate records and meet legal obligations. Retention periods vary according to the type of information and why it was collected. We securely delete or anonymise information when it is no longer needed.

  6. Cookies and similar technologies Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to make it work, understand how it is used, or support other features. Where required, we will ask for your consent before using non-essential cookies. You can manage cookies through your browser settings and, where available, our cookie settings tool. Disabling some cookies may affect website functionality.

  7. Your rights Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request access to your personal information, have it corrected or erased, restrict or object to certain processing, receive certain information in a portable format, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise a right, contact us using the details described in section 1. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

  8. Security We use appropriate measures to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

  9. Children Our services are intended for a general audience. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us online, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

  10. Changes to this policy We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will be made available through our usual customer information channels, with the updated date shown at the top.
